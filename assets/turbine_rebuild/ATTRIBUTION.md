## Rejected / Inaccessible Assets

The following sources were evaluated but could not be downloaded or had blocking restrictions:

### Sketchfab Steam Turbine Models
- **Status:** Download-blocked by authors; view-only or login-gated.
- **Reason:** Most steam turbine models on Sketchfab (e.g., by CanopyCreative, landinweaver) have disabled downloads or require account login. Evaluated for visual reference only.
- **Alternative:** Use ambientCG/Poly Haven textures and GrabCAD CAD components instead.

### TurboSquid Free 3D Models
- **Status:** Requires account login; editorial use restrictions on many free models.
- **Reason:** TurboSquid licensing (Royalty Free / Editorial Use) often restricts commercial product integration; unclear terms for embedded web visualization.
- **Alternative:** Use native Babylon.js-friendly glTF exports from ambientCG/Poly Haven.

### Tripo AI 3D Models
- **Status:** Requires account login and generation credits; proprietary subscription model.
- **Reason:** AI-generated blockouts require paid export credits; commercial use terms tied to Tripo AI's business model.
- **Alternative:** Model custom components in Blender against API 611 / engineering references.
